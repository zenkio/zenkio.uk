Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

This policy covers zenkio.uk and its subdomains (together, "zenkio", "we", "us"), including tools such as Ho & Duck. We build small, free tools and try to collect as little about you as possible.

Information we collect

Our sites don't require an account to use. We don't knowingly collect personal information such as your name or email address unless you choose to give it to us directly (for example, by emailing us).

Cookies and advertising

Some pages on zenkio.uk and its subdomains show ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners use cookies and similar technologies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ad Settings, or opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info.

Some pages may also use basic analytics cookies to understand aggregate traffic, such as how many people visit a page.

Your rights

If you're in the UK or EEA, you have rights under UK/EU data protection law, including the right to know what data is held about you and to ask for it to be corrected or deleted. Because we collect very little personal data directly, most requests will relate to advertising cookies — see the opt-out links above.

Children's privacy

Our sites are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "last updated" date.
